**The wording of 23a(ii) outruns the text.** The dispatch words (ii) as "promised in eternity, formally established in the new covenant; covenant of redemption distinct". What Coxe says is narrower:

- The covenant of redemption is the eternal transaction (II §10).
- The covenant of grace is revealed *by promise from the fall* (II §11), not "promised in eternity".
- It is *fully revealed and furnished with its ordinances* after the old covenant (V §7). He does not use the words "formally established".

A label closer to the located text would be: "covenant of redemption eternal and distinct; covenant of grace revealed by promise from the fall and fully revealed and established in the new covenant." That is the architect's call. This file proposes the locators against whichever wording is ratified.

**The 23b label.** The dispatch's "dual-aspect reading of Gen 17" is the architect's paraphrase. Coxe's own term is a covenant of peculiarity with the natural seed, added to and subservient to the covenant of grace, which was renewed to Abraham in Gen 17:4–5 (V §8).
