## Proposed locators for validity position (4)

Position (4): "(2) plus an administering church of OUR order, by identity or succession — Landmark".

| Locator | What it carries | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Ch. VI, p. 64 | Baptism is to be administered by a specific body to persons with specific qualifications. If any of these is wanting, the act is null. Epigraph texts: Acts 19:3; Rom 6:3; Heb 10:22–23. | [V] |
| Ch. X, pp. 123–124 | No unbaptized man ever had authority to originate baptism, or a church, de novo. The kingdom has stood unbroken since Christ set it up (Matt 16:18; Heb 12:28; Dan 2:44). | [V] p. 124 |
| Ch. X, p. 122 | Disclaims apostolic succession, and the succession of any particular church. | [R] |
| Ch. VI, pp. 66–67 | The Ephesian disciples. One defect listed is that they had no authorized administrator. Graves reads Acts 19:5 as a rebaptism by Paul, the reading Gill rejects (see dispatch §6 CAUTION). | [R] |
| Ch. VI, p. 79 | Conclusions: other denominations' immersions are not to be endorsed as valid. | [R] |
| Ch. IV, pp. 48–49 | The church alone authorizes baptism; a minister may not baptize on his own authority. | [R] |
| Ch. XVII, pp. 205–209 | Endorses the Philadelphia Association's rejection of baptism by unimmersed administrators. | [R] |

**Reading.** Graves identifies a true church by the marks of the model church, which are doctrine and order (Chs. II–VII). What makes him position (4), not (3), is Ch. X: the kingdom's unbroken continuity, and no church gathered de novo by the unbaptized (p. 124). He disclaims succession of particular churches and declines to prove each link (pp. 122–125). The Argument for (4) should cite p. 124, with p. 64 as its secondary locator. This reading is relevant to the ruling on the BSB foreword ([../handoff/PROPOSAL_validity_bsb_foreword.md](../handoff/PROPOSAL_validity_bsb_foreword.md)).

**Acts 19 caution.** Any Argument for (4) from Acts 19:5 depends on Graves's reading, that Paul rebaptized. The Argument must say so (dispatch §6).
