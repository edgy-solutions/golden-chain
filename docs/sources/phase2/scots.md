# Source report: the Scots Confession, 1560

Phase 2 review, added by the ruling on report 19 (A-5: "Scots Confession: add the review (Schaff, Creeds vol. 3)"). Worked 2026-11-17. No text is stored here. Tags: [V] seen on a page image; [R] from a secondary source or the scan's OCR, used for locating only; [U] unknown.

## Edition and public domain
- **Witness proposed:** Philip Schaff, *The Creeds of Christendom*, vol. 3, *The Evangelical Protestant Creeds*. New York: Harper. The archive.org metadata gives 1878 and notes that the stated date is 1877.
  - Scan: archive.org `creedsofchristen187803scha`. Princeton Theological Seminary copy (B. B. Warfield's), marked NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT. [V metadata]
  - The same volume is already the witness for the Belgic articles ([belgic.md](belgic.md)).
- **Public domain:**
  - US: printed before 1930.
  - UK: Schaff died 1893.
  - The 1560 text and the 1572 Latin are centuries out of any term.
- **Heading:** "Confessio Fidei Scoticana I. The Scotch Confession of Faith. A.D. 1560", pp. 437–479. [V: n452 = p. 437, opening and preface; n494 = p. 479, close, with the ratification line of 17 August 1560]
- **Layout:** two columns, the old-spelling Scots and the Latin. Schaff's source note names Dunlop's *Collection of Confessions* (1719, 1722), vol. II, pp. 13 sqq., and Skene's printing of the Acts. [V] The Latin is the 1572 version (Adamson). [R, Schaff's note; not checked on the image]
- **Proof texts:** numbered footnotes throughout. [V]

## Structure and locator scheme
- Twenty-five chapters [R], headed "Art. N", each with a Scots and a Latin title. [V for the ones seen]
- No paragraph numbers. **Locator:** `scots-1560/{article}`, cited with Schaff's printed page.
- **Leaf map:** BookReader n = page + 15. It is the same as for the Belgic articles in this volume, checked on images; see [belgic.md](belgic.md) for why it holds across the deleted leaves.

## Proposed locators (headings only; unread) [U]
- **The Church and its marks:** Arts. XVI and XVIII; (XXV not yet read for this.)
- **The administrator of the sacraments:** Art. XXII. Its heading is on the right administration; it is the natural place to test a "lawful minister" requirement against the Baptist confessions.
- **Who may receive the sacraments:** Art. XXIII, which bears on infant baptism.
- **The law and works:** Arts. XIII–XV. Only XV was located; XIII and XIV are [U].
- **The magistrate:** Art. XXIV.

These are proposals from headings. The architect reads the text before any Position cites them.
