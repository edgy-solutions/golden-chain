# Source report: John Gill on justification and the administrator of baptism (locators in the chosen edition)

Phase 2 source review (review only). Research done 2026-11-15. Extends `docs/sources/gill-body-of-divinity.md` and `gill-divine-right.md`; does not repeat them. No text stored. Nothing minted.

Tags: **[V]** fetched and inspected (contents/headings); **[R]** secondary; **[U]** unknown.

## Edition and public domain

- Edition already chosen: Baptist Standard Bearer 2019 (reprint of the Tegg 1839 London one-volume quarto), working text. PD scan witness: Tegg 1839 on archive.org (`completebodyofdo01gill`, `completebodyofdo02gill`). Both as in the existing report. Gill d. 1771, PD worldwide; first editions 1769 (Doctrinal) and 1770 (Practical). No translator.
- Nothing new on PD. BSB front matter remains excluded.
- Locating aid only: CCEL `https://ccel.org/ccel/gill/doctrinal` and `.../practical`. Practical III.1 is CCEL file `practical.iv.i.html` (CCEL's own numbering; not Gill's). [V]

## Proposed locator scheme

`Doctrinal {Book}.{Chapter}` and `Practical {Book}.{Chapter}`, with Gill's own book numbers as printed in BSB (Doctrinal I-VII, Practical I-IV), and section as Gill's own ordinal heads ("First", "Secondly", ...) used as `§n`. Pages are an optional secondary literal (BSB printed page, or Tegg volume and page), never in an IRI. This follows the existing report (`Doctrinal VI.4`, `Practical III.1 §3`).

## Chapters read from the CCEL contents [V]

Doctrinal Book II, "Of the Acts and Works of God" (16 chapters, matching the BSB tally):
- II.1 Internal acts and works of God, decrees in general
- II.2 Special decrees, election; II.3 Rejection; II.4 Eternal union of the elect
- **II.5 Other eternal and immanent acts in God, particularly adoption and justification**
- II.6 Everlasting council; II.7 Everlasting covenant of grace; II.8 part of the Father; II.9 part of the Son; II.10 Christ as head; II.11 mediator; II.12 surety; II.13 testator; II.14 concern of the Spirit; II.15 properties of the covenant; II.16 complacency of the persons

Doctrinal Book VI, "Of the Blessings of Grace, and the Doctrines of It" (15 chapters): VI.1 Redemption; VI.2 causes; VI.3 objects; VI.4 texts on universal redemption; VI.5 satisfaction; VI.6 propitiation, atonement, reconciliation; **VI.7 Of the pardon of sin; VI.8 Of justification**; VI.9 adoption; VI.10 liberty of the sons of God; VI.11 regeneration; VI.12 effectual calling; VI.13 conversion; VI.14 sanctification; VI.15 perseverance.

Practical Book III, "Of the Public Ordinances of Divine Worship" (8 chapters): **III.1 Of Baptism**; III.2 Lord's Supper; III.3 public ministry of the word; III.4 hearing; III.5 prayer; III.6 Lord's Prayer; III.7 singing psalms; III.8 place and time. Practical Book II, "On External Worship, as Public" (6 chapters): II.1 gospel church; II.2 duties of members; II.3 officers, particularly pastors; II.4 duties to pastors; II.5 deacons; II.6 discipline.

**Discrepancy to settle on the BSB contents (PDF pp. 16-17):** a second reading of the Tegg v.1 OCR contents gave Book II as 17 chapters, with adoption and justification as separate chapters (II.5, II.6). That conflicts with CCEL (one chapter II.5) and with the ratified 16-chapter tally. The OCR reading is probably a mis-split; treat CCEL and the BSB tally as correct until the image is looked at. [U]

## Separate tracts on baptism

- No separate tract on **who may administer baptism** found. A forum thread reports that Gill, in the Practical Divinity chapter, argues that one who may preach may baptize (secondary, unread). [R]
- On file: `gill-divine-right.md` (Divine Right of Infant-Baptism Examined, 1749).
- Other baptism titles reported only by title: *Baptism a Divine Command to be Observed*; *Baptism a Public Ordinance of Divine Worship*; *The Ancient Mode of Baptizing*; *An Answer to a Welsh Clergyman's Twenty Arguments*; *A Reply to a Book entitled a Defence of the Divine Right of Infant Baptism* (against Clark). Transcriptions on sermonindex.net carry no licence; locating only. None examined; whether any addresses the administrator is [U]. Collected in *Sermons and Tracts* (1814-15), not seen. [R]
