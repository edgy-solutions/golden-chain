## The work and public domain

- Latin original: *De oeconomia foederum Dei cum hominibus libri quatuor* (first printing 1677 [U, not checked]; later Latin editions 1685, 1694, 1712, 1739). Four books. [R; 1685, 1739 scans seen in IA listings, V]
- Witsius (1636-1708). English translation by William Crookshank (IA creator record: 1712?-1769), first English printing London: Edward Dilly, 1763 (stated date 1762 on the title page per IA note). [V catalogue]
- **PD:** author d. 1708 and translator d. 1769, so PD worldwide (UK life+70, US pre-1929). No later editor's text is needed. A modern reprint (den Dulk, 1990, Packer introduction, from the 1822 text [R]) and Reformation Heritage Books (2010) are copyrighted as editions; never use. Packer's introduction is copyrighted.
- The translation is Crookshank's, revised by him; the 1798 New York and 1804 editions add a life of Witsius by Marck [R, Online Books Page].
