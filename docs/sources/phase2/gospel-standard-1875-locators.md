# Gospel Standard Articles in the 1875 Gadsby printing: proposed locators and headings (overnight order item 8)

Proposals only. Nothing is minted, and the 1875 printing is not yet a Source in the data: it waits on the inclusion console (item 6) and Chris's confirmation of the title page and the I-XXXI span.
- **Seen:** on page images of the private-corpus PDF, rendered by the build agent (PDF pages 14-18 = printed pp. 11-15; printed page = PDF page - 3).
- **Not used:** OCR or the PDF's text layer.
- **Text:** no article text is stored. The headings are the build agent's own short descriptions, with at most a few of the printed words in quotation marks. Chris checks each against the pixels before anything is minted.
- **Proposed locator scheme:** `Gospel Standard Articles (1875 Gadsby printing), Art. N, p. P`. Articles are numbered in roman numerals in the print. The locator uses arabic numerals, as the order did ("Articles 24, 26, 29").
