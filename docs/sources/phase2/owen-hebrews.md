## The work and public domain

- Owen (1616–1683). The *Exposition* first appeared in four folio volumes, 1668–1684. The third volume is *A Continuation of the Exposition… on the Sixth, Seventh, Eighth, Ninth and Tenth Chapters* (London, 1680). Coxe's preface (1681) calls it "Dr. Owen's 3d Vol. upon the Hebrews". [R; Coxe preface V]
- **PD:** author d. 1683. The Goold edition (1854–55) adds an editor's notes; Goold d. 1891, so it is PD worldwide.
