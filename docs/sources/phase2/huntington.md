## Author and public domain

- William Huntington (1745-1813), signing "S.S." Died 1813, so PD worldwide (UK and US). The 1786/1788 printings and the 1811 Works are all pre-1929. Modern reprints, selections and annotated editions are not to be used; they may carry copyrighted apparatus.

## The work the brief names: *The Law Established by the Faith of Christ*

- **It is a sermon, not a treatise.** Preached at Providence Chapel on 1 January 1786, printed 1786 and 1788, with a dedicatory letter to the congregation. [V catalogue and title]
- IA copies (eighteenth-century collection, ESTC-derived):
