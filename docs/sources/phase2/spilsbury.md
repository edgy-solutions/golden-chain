# Source report: John Spilsbury (Spilsbery), 1643 treatise and the 1644 First London Confession

Phase 2 review only. Research 2026-11-15. No text stored. Tags: [V] fetched; [R] secondary; [U] unknown.

## Works
- *A Treatise concerning the Lawfull Subject of Baptisme*, London, "Printed in the yeare, 1643", publisher not identified. Wing **S4976**; ESTC R23657. [R, OTA, Penn and TAMU catalogue records] The brief's S5017 is **not** supported by any record found.
- 2nd ed., "Corrected and enlarged by the Author", London: Henry Hills, Fleet-yard, **1652**; ESTC R219122; 74 pp. [R, ESTC mirror and TAMU]. Wing number for 1652 unconfirmed (S4977 is unsupported). [U]
- Signatory to the 1644 *First London Confession* (Particular Baptists, seven churches). [R] The 1644 printing is not reviewed here; its Wing number and the article numbers of its baptism articles are [U].
- Spilsbury c. 1593 to c. 1668 [R, Wikipedia]. PD by age in US and UK.

## Witness proposed
The 1643 first edition (TCP A61127 for locating; the architect's EEBO access for verifying). The 1652 is a revised and enlarged edition, so a **separate witness**, not a variant. Which one represents his view is for the architect. [U]
