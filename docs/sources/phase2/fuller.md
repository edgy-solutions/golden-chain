## The work

- 2nd ed. **1801**, revised and enlarged; later title *The Gospel Worthy of All Acceptation, or the Duty of Sinners to Believe in Jesus Christ*. Sources differ on the 1801 subtitle. [R]
- Written c. 1778-81 (sources differ). [R]
- Related, not the target: *A Defence of a Treatise ...* (1787, reply to Button); Dan Taylor, *Observations* (1786). [R]
