## The work and its dates (sources conflict; unresolved)
- Articles of the Gospel Standard Aid and Poor Relief Societies; now **35 articles**, with a Declaration of Truth and Rules. [R]
- baptists.net: amended by W. Gadsby, J. M'Kenzie and J. C. Philpot; "in 1878" agreed by a committee of nine; then adopted by a general meeting of the Societies. [R]
- reformedwiki.org, citing Matrunola et al. (2011), *Articles of Faith: Why and where from?*, p. 14: 31 articles published **1872**; **arts. 32-35 added April 1879**. [R]
- A chapel site dates its text "1888 & 1991", with a footnote at Art. XXVI citing *Gospel Standard* for Dec. 1906. [R]
- **The dates asked about for arts. 26-33 are not confirmed.** Only this: 1-31 older (1872 or 1878), 32-35 later (1879, one source), footnotes later still. [U]
