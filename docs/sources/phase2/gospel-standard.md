# Source report: Gospel Standard Articles of Faith (Strict Baptist)

Phase 2 review only. Research 2026-11-15. No text stored. Tags: [V] fetched; [R] secondary; [U] unknown.

## The work and its dates (sources conflict; unresolved)
- Articles of the Gospel Standard Aid and Poor Relief Societies; now **35 articles**, with a Declaration of Truth and Rules. [R]
- baptists.net: amended by W. Gadsby, J. M'Kenzie and J. C. Philpot; "in 1878" agreed by a committee of nine; then adopted by a general meeting of the Societies. [R]
- reformedwiki.org, citing Matrunola et al. (2011), *Articles of Faith: Why and where from?*, p. 14: 31 articles published **1872**; **arts. 32-35 added April 1879**. [R]
- A chapel site dates its text "1888 & 1991", with a footnote at Art. XXVI citing *Gospel Standard* for Dec. 1906. [R]
- **The dates asked about for arts. 26-33 are not confirmed.** Only this: 1-31 older (1872 or 1878), 32-35 later (1879, one source), footnotes later still. [U]

## Article numbers and short headings (as printed on a chapel site, Salem Chapel; later wording) [R]
XX Growth in Grace; XXI Indwelling Sin; XXII Backsliding and Chastening; XXIII Final Perseverance; XXIV Gospel Invitations; XXV Universal Redemption Denied; XXVI Duty Faith and Duty Repentance Denied; XXVII The Non-Elect Incapable of Receiving Grace; XXVIII Baxterianism Denied; XXIX Indiscriminate Offers of Grace Denied; XXX Christ's Glorified Body; XXXI Annihilation of the Wicked Denied; XXXII Preaching of the Gospel (apostolic); XXXIII Preaching to the Unconverted; XXXIV Preaching of the Gospel (exhorting the unregenerate); XXXV Degrees of Faith.
These are the headings of the modern text; an early printing must be compared. [U]
