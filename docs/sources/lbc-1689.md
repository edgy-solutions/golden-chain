## The work

- First published in London in 1677 as *A Confession of Faith put forth by the Elders and Brethren of many Congregations of Christians, Baptized upon Profession of their Faith*.
- Reprinted in 1688, and endorsed by the General Assembly of Particular Baptists in London in 1689. [R; the title wording and the 1688/1689 dates were seen in the 1719 edition's front matter, V]
- Derived from the Savoy Declaration (1658), itself a revision of WCF. [R]
- **32 chapters**, with numbered paragraphs. [V]
- Paragraph counts: ch. 7 = 3; ch. 28 = 2; ch. 29 = 4; ch. 30 = 8. [V]
- **Chapter numbers differ from WCF.** LBC inserts ch. 20 "Of the Gospel", so WCF 26 → LBC 27, WCF 27 → LBC 28, WCF 28 → LBC 29, and WCF 29 → LBC 30. [V from the LBC contents]
  - The WCF↔LBC chapter concordance is data, not identity. It is never encoded in IRIs.

### 1677 vs 1689

- No scan or transcription of the 1677 first printing was found online, and no collation of 1677/1688/1689. [U]
- **Witness of record (architect ruling, 2026-10-25): the 1677 first printing, facsimile edition (2000).** The architect read the proof-text notes of "Of Baptism" (sig. H) from it. The slug stays `lbc-1689`; the edition is recorded on `B source/lbc-1689` as `dcterms:bibliographicCitation`.
- The text is described as unchanged since 1677. [R, no collation seen]
- `B body/lbc-1689` names the 1689 endorsement, which is how the confession is known and adopted.
