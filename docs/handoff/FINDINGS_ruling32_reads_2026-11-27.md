| | Answer |
|---|---|
| **C-d** | "There be onely two Sacraments ordained by Christ our Lord in the Gospel; that is to say, Baptism and the Supper of the Lord: neither of which may be dispensed by any, but by a Minister of the Word lawfully ordained." (27.4, n54-55). |

## R3. Fuller 1785: "offer"

- **Searched:** IA's OCR of the whole 1785 volume (215 leaves), for "offer" and its forms, including the long-s and line-split forms.
- **Found:** 11 uses. **None is of the gospel offered to sinners.**
  - Four are of a thing "offered to consideration" or "offered" for perusal (n5, n11, n92 p. 77, n162 p. 145).
  - One is of Christ who "offered himself" (n26, p. 11).
  - Five are of sacrifice or offering (n107 p. 92, twice, one in a scriptural note; n126 p. 111, twice; n128 p. 113).
  - One is of "every opportunity that offers" (n203, p. 186).
- **Its limit:** the OCR is not a witness, and a word it misread would not be found. So the search shows only that no use was **located**. It does not prove that there is none.
- **Result, as the ruling put it:** no other use was found, so Fuller's "proposal of peace" (p. 162) stands as his own wording. G-P3 is unchanged.

## R4. A Gill lead on duty-faith (reported; not supplied to any cell)

- **Searched:** IA's OCR of *The Cause of God and Truth*, Parts I-IV (IA `bim_eighteenth-century_the-cause-of-god-and-tru_gill-john_1735_1` to `_4`), for "duty", "obliged", "bound" and "required" within eight words of "believe in Christ" (or "in him"), and for "not the duty of", "duty of all men", "duty to believe", "special faith" and "saving faith is".
- **Each lead below was then read on the page image.**

| # | Locator | What it says, in Gill's words |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Part I, Numb. XXVIII (on John i. 7), p. 149 (n156) | "II. That the obligation to believe in Christ, and so the faith to which men are obliged, are in proportion, and according to the nature of the revelation of the gospel, which obliges them." |
| L1 | the same, p. 151 (n158) | "only such to whom this revelation is made, and are capable of hearing and understanding it, are obliged to faith in Christ by it;" |
| L1 | the same, p. 152 (n159) | "Now, according to the revelation is the faith men are obliged to, and what is produced by it: If the revelation is external, or the gospel comes in word only, the faith men are obliged to is only an historical one; nor can any other follow upon it; and that Christ died for every individual man, is no part of the revelation." |
| L2 | Part III, p. 68 (n71), Gill's reply to Whitby's objection that none to whom salvation was never intended "are bound to believe in him" | "I reply; The consequence is very just; none are bound to believe in Christ, but such to whom a revelation of him is made; and according to the revelation, is the faith they are obliged to." |

- **What the build agent does not decide:**
