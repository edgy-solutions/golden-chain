## P3. Particular leads

- **P3.1** LC Q. 32 calls faith "the condition", in the 1658 printing as in later ones. WCF 11 does not use the word "condition".
- **P3.2** LBC 11.4 adds "personally" to the clause on when the elect are justified, where WCF 11.4 does not have it.
- **P3.3** Gill's sub-entry on justification as an immanent act is at or near BSB p. 203, and in it he calls justification by faith a declaration or manifestation, in the conscience, of the act from eternity.
- **P3.4** Gill's Book VI chapter on justification is VI.8 and begins at BSB p. 501.
- **P3.5** Witsius III.8 is in vol. 2 of the 1763 set.
- **P3.6** Heidelberg Q. 61 speaks of receiving and applying, not of an instrument.
- **P3.7** Belgic Art. XXII calls faith an instrument.
- **P3.8** The proposal will have between 4 and 6 Positions, each worded only from the sources.
