# Ruling: the site's ground (filed verbatim; draft only)

- **From:** the architect, on Chris's decision of 2026-11-22, relayed by Chris, 2026-11-22.
- **Status:** draft only. Nothing is built until it is ratified. It supersedes the earlier draft of this block.

```
ARCHITECT, on Chris's decision of 2026-11-22 (supersedes the
earlier draft of this block). Draft only; build nothing until
ratified.

The site's ground is three commitments: (1) Scripture as sole
standard, every Position with proof texts; (2) the scope of
published documents included; (3) accuracy to each source's own
words and page. body/plumb-line IS the site's standard, authored
and signed by Chris. It is stated with humility: a standing
statement, in Chris's words and signed by him, says the standard
is fallible, Scripture is its judge, and the witnesses are shown
so the reader can test it. Display wording stays "by this site's
standard".

1. ADR-0003 Revision 3 (proposed): the site's judgments come from
   body/plumb-line. weigh may also be run from another Body or a
   visitor's stances as a comparison view, labelled as such.
   gc:unchurches may be carried by any Body with a dcterms:source;
   stated and derived judgments are distinct and displayed
   distinctly. The humility statement appears wherever the site's
   standard is applied; its text is Chris's, on a console.
2. ADR-0009 (proposed): scope of documents, who decides, how shown.
3. Warrant: recorded only from the source's own claim, with
   locator; unstated stays unstated; an opponent's characterisation
   is the opponent's Argument. Add the warrant locus (WCF 1.6; LBC
   1.6, read on the page) to the phase-2 table.
4. Phase-3 ADR (proposed): church accounts, who speaks by polity,
   verification recorded, statement and testimony separate,
   testimony private by default, the three gates.
Send all four up for review.
```
