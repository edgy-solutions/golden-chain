## P3. Particular leads

- **P3.1** Scott's Dort II.5 says the promise is to be published to all "promiscuously" or "without distinction", together with the command to repent and believe.
- **P3.2** Dort III/IV.9 speaks of Christ "offered" in the gospel.
- **P3.3** WCF 7.3 and LBC 7.2 (1719) have the same phrase, "freely offereth unto sinners".
- **P3.4** Fuller 1785 sets out the duty of faith as numbered propositions or arguments in a Part of its own, with a separate Part answering objections.
- **P3.5** Gill CGT treats the invitations in Part I, section by section on the passages, and uses the word "offer" only to qualify or deny it.
- **P3.6** No GS75 cell is answered. Arts. 10-13 and 23 do not say any of G-a to G-e in their own words.
