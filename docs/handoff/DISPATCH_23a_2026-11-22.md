== THE TEST ==
"Were the elect in view?" separates nobody; every author says yes.
Three senses must be kept apart:
  1. in view, as beneficiaries (all affirm);
  2. party as contractors (none affirm);
  3. party in their head: Christ covenants as representative of the
     elect, so they are in the covenant in him from eternity and
     its blessings are theirs from then.
The dividing line is between 1 and 3. Put these questions to each
source and answer each from its own words, with page:
  Q-a. Does he say the elect were in the eternal covenant in Christ
       as their head, so that its blessings were theirs from
       eternity? Or that it was made for them, their own covenant
       standing beginning in time?
  Q-b. Does he treat the covenant of redemption and the covenant of
       grace as one covenant or as two?
  Q-c. When does he say the covenant of grace with man begins or is
       made: in eternity, at the promise after the fall, or in the
       new covenant?
An answer is a quotation of at most three sentences with its
locator, or "does not say in the sections read". Never a paraphrase
offered as his view.
