## Proposal: B, KEEP, RESTATED

**Restated (ii), in Owen's words** (vol. 19, Exerc. XXVIII §2, p. 78), cited to Owen:

> Two covenants: "the covenant that he made with his Son concerning men", "the personal compact that was between the Father and the Son before the world was"; and "the covenant that God made with men concerning Christ", which "after the fall he entered into… with mankind", "commonly called the covenant of grace".

This is assembled only from Owen's quotations. Chris signs or corrects the label. The IRI `position/covenant-of-grace/established-in-new-covenant` stays (IRIs never change), though its slug no longer fits the label.

**For B.**
- Owen states the distinction in so many words (Q-b, two). Gill states the contrary in so many words and names the view he rejects: "some divines, indeed, make them distinct covenants… but this is very wrongly said" (p. 217).
- The two answers to Q-a part along the dispatch's line between sense 1 and sense 3:
  - Gill: "with the elect in him, as their Head and Representative"; blessings "secured in that eternal compact" (pp. 216–217).
  - Owen: "through faith, was imputed unto them that did believe" (p. 88); the covenant of grace is entered "with believers" (p. 90).

**Against A (merge).** (ii), as Owen states it, asserts two covenants. (i) asserts one, and Gill denies the two by name. A merge would join a claim and its denial.

**Against C (move).** The parting is in the covenant chapter itself (Q-b), before justification is reached. A difference on justification does follow:
- Gill II.5 p. 203: justification "does not begin to take place in time, or at believing";
- Owen p. 88: "through faith, was imputed".

That passage pair is noted for the phase-2 justification locus as **related**, not as the place where 23a parts.

**Who holds restated (ii)? For Chris to judge, not proposed:**
- **Coxe.** Q-b is "does not say". He has the covenant of redemption and the grace "in pursuance" of it "upon the Fall" (II §10), but also headship language (I §5). The quotations are above.
- **LBC 1677.** The covenant of grace is made after the fall (7.2) and "founded in" an eternal transaction (7.3). "Two" is not said. Compare Gill's "foundation" language.
- **Owen** is not a Source in the data. Holding him on (ii) needs his inclusion under ADR-0009, by its batch console.

**Chris's observation (recorded, attached to no Position):** Owen §15 (p. 90) puts "the prevision" of the fall "in order of nature antecedent unto this covenant". That bears on the infralapsarian side of the resemblance Chris noted. It is noted here only.
