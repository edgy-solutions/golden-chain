## L3. The proposal

- **C: a third Position, "a minister called by a gospel church".** Gill ties the administrator to a church by the minister's call, not by the church acting in the baptism, and he does not require a baptized administrator or a succession.
- "Not a church ordinance" will **not** be found to mean that no church is needed.
