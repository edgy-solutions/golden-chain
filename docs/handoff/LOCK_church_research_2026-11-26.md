## P3. Particular leads

- **P3.1** WCF 25.2 has "together with their children". LBC 26.2 has no clause on children.
- **P3.2** LBC 26.6 says the members "willingly consent" to walk together.
- **P3.3** Belgic XXIX and Scots XVIII each name three marks: preaching of the word, the sacraments rightly administered, and discipline.
- **P3.4** Both WCF 25.5 and LBC 26.3 have the phrase "Synagogues of Satan".
- **P3.5** WCF 31 gives synods a power to determine controversies "ministerially". LBC 26.15 lets churches meet by messengers to advise, but gives those messengers no jurisdiction or church-power over the churches.
- **P3.6** Belgic XXXI has ministers, elders and deacons chosen by "lawful election" of the church.
- **P3.7** Gill Practical II.1 forms a gospel church by the members' consent or confederation, and Book II gives two kinds of officer: pastors (or elders) and deacons.
- **P3.8** Book II gives no passage that contradicts report 25's answers to Q1 (a minister of the word administers) or Q2 (ordinarily, a church's call).
- **P3.9** The proposal will have between 5 and 8 Positions, each worded only from the sources.
