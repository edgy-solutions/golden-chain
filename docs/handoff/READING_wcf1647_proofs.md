# Reading form: WCF 1647 proof notes as printed (ruling Q16)

For: Chris, who signs each row from the page images of the witness, in one sitting.

The build agent may view these public-domain page images to propose strings (ruling on report 6, Q4). A proposed string binds nothing until you sign it from the page. Where you differ, write what is printed.

- **Witness:** Edinburgh, Evan Tyler, 1647. IA `humbleadviceofas00west`, page images at `archive.org/details/humbleadviceofas00west`. The leaves are listed per chapter below. The page is the head as printed on the leaf, recorded in `gc:asPrinted`; the locator is the IA leaf number; where a head is misprinted, the note carries "leaf − 8 gives N" (ruling, report 7 Q3). Leaf − 8 fails on leaves 24 and 55 (they print (14) and (46)). See [../sources/westminster.md](../sources/westminster.md), § "Proof texts as printed".
- **What to fill:** for each Argument, the letter or letters of the note, the note exactly as printed, and the printed page.
  - Keep the original spelling, punctuation and spacing, including "with" and "&". A wrapped line is joined with one space; all other spacing is kept as printed (ruling, report 7 Q4).
  - Write long s as s.
- **One note or several:** if the texts below fall under more than one letter, give one row per letter. The Argument then splits (ADR-0005: one Argument per lettered note).
- **Mismatches:** if the 1647 note prints texts that Schaff does not, or omits some, write what is printed and mark it.

The "Schaff (A)" column is a reading aid only, from the 1889 apparatus. The "OCR letter" column is the letter that the 1647 OCR shows at that clause. It is unverified.
