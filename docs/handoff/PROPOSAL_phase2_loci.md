# Proposal: phase-2 locus table (overnight order 2026-11-15, item 5)

- **Status:** PROPOSED. A document only. Nothing is minted, no IRI is
  reserved, and no row is in the data.
- **For:** the architect (questions, Positions, splits) and Chris (signing).
- **Sources:** the item-4 reviews in [docs/sources/phase2/](../sources/phase2/README.md)
  and the phase-1 source files in [docs/sources/](../sources/).

## How to read this table

- **The question** is worded by the order. Where the order gave sub-questions,
  they are kept.
- **Candidate Positions** are the build agent's wording of the answers the
  sources are known to argue about. They are drafts for the architect to
  reword, merge or split. They are not claims about what any source says.
- **"Where to look"** is a locator from a source review. It says where a
  source for that Position is expected, by heading or catalogue only. **No
  locator here has been read for its content**, so none of them is a citation
  yet. Under rule 2, each one becomes an Argument only after the page is read
  and the row is signed.
- A cell marked **none located** means that no item-4 source was found for that
  Position. The Position may still be real.

## 1. Faith and justification

**Question.** Is faith the instrument of justification, or the reception of it?
When is a sinner justified: in eternity, at Christ's resurrection, or upon
believing?

| Candidate Position | Where to look | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1a. Faith is the instrument; justified upon believing | WCF 11.1–2 ([westminster.md](../sources/westminster.md)); LBC 11.1–2 ([lbc-1689.md](../sources/lbc-1689.md)); Witsius III.8 ([witsius.md](../sources/phase2/witsius.md)) | chapter headings [V]; content unread |
| 1b. Justified in eternity (an immanent act in God); faith receives or evidences it | Gill, Doctrinal II.5 and VI.8 ([gill-justification.md](../sources/phase2/gill-justification.md)); Huntington, *The Grace of God in the Justification of a Sinner* ([huntington.md](../sources/phase2/huntington.md)) | Gill titles [V]; Huntington by title only [U] |
| 1c. Justified at Christ's resurrection, as the head of the elect | none located | — |
| 1d. Justification in stages (decreed, accomplished, applied), with faith as the reception of the last | Gill, Doctrinal VI.8 (sections unread) | [U] |

**A split the architect may want:** "instrument or reception" and "when" are
two questions. 1a–1d mix them because the sources do. Separate Loci (1-i: what
faith does; 1-ii: when) would let a Body hold "instrument" and "eternal"
together, if any does.

**Seed of a future plumb-line Argument on this row, recorded verbatim,
unsigned.** Chris, 2026-11-15:

> "The act of justifying can be thus determined before its effected as the judge deliberates the verdict in closed quarters and then delivers it publicly."

It is his to sign later. It is not attached to a Position here: which of 1a–1d
it supports is for Chris to say when he signs it.
